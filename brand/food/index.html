Policies

Good to know

Allergies and dietary needs?

Every item is labeled. Tell us about allergies when you book, or have each guest add them through their RSVP link. Our kitchen isn't allergen-free, so ask us about cross-contact. confirm

Can we bring a birthday cake?

Yes. Bring a store-bought or bakery cake, and we'll provide plates, candles and a lighter. set policy

Can we bring other outside food?

Only birthday cakes, please. Everything else comes from our kitchen.

Can kids order?

Yes. The kitchen menu works for all ages, and the bar is 21+ with ID.
