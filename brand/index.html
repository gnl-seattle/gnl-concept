How it works

Book it. Pick a coaster.
Play till someone wins.

Step 1 · Book
"Who's free Friday?"

Pick a night and how long you want to play, from 1 to 3 hours. One person books the lounge and pays for two spots to hold it. Everyone else pays for their own when they arrive.

Step 2 · Pick a coaster
"I'm the shark. Obviously."

Choose yours from a wall of 500+ coasters. It's your game piece for the night, and it pops up on screen every time you score.

Step 3 · Play
"Wait, how do we buzz in?"

Tap your coaster on the table. Every game teaches itself in about 30 seconds, so nobody reads a rulebook. Pizza and drinks come straight to your lounge.

Step 4 · Crown a champ
"Rematch. Same time next week."

When time's up, someone has bragging rights and someone else is already planning the rematch.
