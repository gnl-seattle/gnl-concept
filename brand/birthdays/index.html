Parents ask

The fine print

What ages is this for?

The games are built for ages 6 and up. Kids 5 and under get in free, but they need a ticket to register a coaster and play. add ratings

Can we bring a cake?

Yes. Bring a store-bought or bakery cake, and we'll provide plates, candles and a lighter. Please tell us about any allergies when you book. set policy

Allergies and dietary needs?

Menu items are labeled vegetarian, gluten-free and nut-free. Tell us ahead of time and we'll plan around them. confirm

Do parents stay?

We need one adult for every 8 kids. Grown-ups can relax on the sectional or join a round.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekend afternoons fill up first, so book 3–4 weeks ahead. Weekday after-school parties are easier to get.
