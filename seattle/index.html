Seattle questions

Good to know

When do you open?

We're aiming for 2027. Waitlist members hear first and get first pick of opening-week dates.

Do I have to drink?

No. The games are the main event, and the zero-proof menu gets as much attention as the cocktails.

Can kids come?

We plan to have family hours during the day. We'll post the final age policy before opening. confirm

How is this different from playing Jackbox at home?

Our games are built for the room: game-show lights and sound, a coffee table you play on, and coasters as game pieces. Plus someone else brings the pizza and does the dishes.

Can we book a whole team?

Yes. Bigger groups get side-by-side lounges with a shared leaderboard, or you can rent the whole venue. See team events.
