How a game nite goes

Friday, 7:40 PM.
The group chat is stuck.

7:40 PM
"Dinner again? Or…"

Someone books a lounge for six and pays a deposit for two tickets. Everyone else pays for their own ticket at the door.

8:02 PM
"I'm the shark. Obviously."

Everyone picks a coaster. It's your game piece, and it's on screen every time you score.

8:10 PM
"Wait, how do we buzz in?"

The lights drop and the show starts. Each game teaches itself in 30 seconds. The pizza arrives during round two.

9:58 PM
"Rematch. Same time next week."

Someone's crowned champion, someone else demands a rematch, and somebody buys a membership.
