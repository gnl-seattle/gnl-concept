House rules

Plan your visit

When should we arrive?

On your first visit, arrive 20 minutes early so everyone can register a coaster before your session timer starts. Returning players need about 10 minutes.

Where do we park?

There's street parking, and a public garage one block away.

Is the lounge private?

Yes. We take one booking per lounge, so it's only your group for the whole session.

How does paying work?

The organizer pays a two-ticket deposit to book. Everyone else pays for their own ticket by card when they arrive.

What ages can play?

Tickets are for ages 6 and up. Kids 5 and under get in free, but they need a ticket to register a coaster and play.
