For planners & procurement

The paperwork, answered

Can you invoice us?

Yes. Pay by corporate card or by invoice. A deposit holds your date. set terms

W-9 and certificate of insurance?

Yes. Our W-9 is available on request, and we can name your company as additional insured on our certificate. confirm limits

What if we need to cancel?

You can reschedule for free up to 14 days before your event. set policy

Can we limit or skip alcohol?

Yes. Choose drink tickets, a capped tab, zero-proof only or no bar at all.

Dietary needs and accessibility?

Menu items are labeled vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free. The lounges are step-free, and game rules appear on screen. confirm
